The point

People choose before they call.

Anyone looking for a lawyer, a doctor, a builder or a supplier looks first: at the website, the profiles, whatever they can find about you. If all they find is a logo and a list of services, they choose whoever let them see a face and hear a voice.

You do your work well. The point is that people who don't know you have no way of knowing it.

Why me

For years I told travel stories on Instagram and reached 30,000 people without spending anything on advertising. That's where I learned what makes someone stop and watch, and what gets scrolled past. I use the same method for professionals: simple videos that explain and reassure.

The processFive steps

How it works

  1. A free twenty-minute call: you tell me what you do, and I tell you honestly whether video is right for you.

  2. A tailored proposal.

  3. I write the scripts, starting from the questions your clients ask.

  4. Half a day of filming at your premises, with my equipment.

  5. Editing and publishing for the period the session covers.

I work alone, so I only take on a few clients at a time.

Upfront

I only make content that builds trust: explanations, recurring cases, answers to frequently asked questions.

No comedy trends or clickbait. For a professional firm, not looking out of place is worth more than a few extra views.

If during the consultation I realise video won't help you, I'll tell you. I'd rather say no straight away than take on work that leads nowhere.
