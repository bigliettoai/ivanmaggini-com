About19, based in Italy

One person, two lines of work.

I do two things. I'm building Biglietto, a product for people who travel. And with Ivan Maggini Studio I produce videos for professional firms and companies across Italy: I go on site to film, then I edit and publish.

Underneath there's only one thing. I spent the last few years telling travel stories on Instagram and got to 30,000 people without spending anything on ads. That's where I learned what makes someone stop and watch and what just scrolls past, and it's where the idea for Biglietto came from.

I don't have a technical background and I don't pretend to. What I can do is start from a problem I've actually had and keep at it until it turns into something that works, saying out loud along the way what doesn't.
