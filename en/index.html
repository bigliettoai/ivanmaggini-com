Welcome

Nice to meet you, I'm Ivan.

I'm 19 and I live in Lurano, a small town near Bergamo. If you're here, either I wrote to you, or you saw one of my trips, or someone told you about me.

For a few years now I've been sharing my travels on Instagram, without spending anything on advertising. That's how I learned what makes someone stop in front of a video, and what makes them scroll past.

Today I use what I learned in two ways.

The first is Biglietto, a product for people who travel. I started it in June, while studying for my final school exams, and I'm building it one piece at a time.

The second is Ivan Maggini Studio, a small video studio I run from Italy.

I don't have a long career to tell you about, and I don't pretend to. I'd rather tell you myself than have you find out.
