Biglietto

I'm building Biglietto: one place where your whole trip lives, and on top of it, the paid part nobody tells you, what to actually buy, in which order, and where.

Where it comes from

Travel is great. Buying it sucks. Your bookings end up scattered across six apps, and the right ticket is almost never the one shown to you first, because whoever is selling it has no reason to mention it.

Where it goes

Where this goes is further out: once something knows your whole trip, it doesn't have to wait for you to ask. An assistant that acts before you notice, not one that answers. That's years of work, and I won't pretend otherwise.

I document all of it every day, including the parts that go badly.
