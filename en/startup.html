The main project is Biglietto: an AI-native travel assistant, live and usable today. It came out of a problem I ran into as a traveller, not out of a market analysis.

Main project

Biglietto

One place for your whole trip. Photograph a ticket, a hotel confirmation, a ferry booking — Biglietto reads it and files it on your trip calendar, in the right place.

What it does today

  • Photograph a ticket and the AI pulls out the details — date, time, route, name.
  • Fix anything it misreads in one tap: you confirm every entry before it's saved.
  • Export everything to the calendar app you already use.
  • See your travel in numbers, not scattered across your inbox.

Where it stands

The product is live: anyone can create their own calendar and start using it. Development happens in public, update after update.

What comes next is the proactive layer — an assistant that surfaces the right train, the right border rule and the right local detail before you think to search for it. That's the part I'm raising early funding for, and looking for a technical co-founder to build.

The site also carries a few free travel guides, written from firsthand experience on the road.
