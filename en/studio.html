One thing I'll say right away.

I only make content that builds authority: explanations, recurring cases, answers to frequently asked questions.

QuestionsThe six most common

The questions I'm asked most often

How much of my time does it take?

Roughly 1–3 hours a month, for the filming sessions. Everything else, from editing to publishing, is my job.

I wouldn't know what to say in front of the camera.

The outline never starts from scratch: the questions you get every week are already the content plan. And during the session I guide you, including with a question-and-answer format if that puts you more at ease.

Who approves the content before it's published?

You do, always. Nothing goes online without your approval, which matters especially for professions with codes of conduct.

Do you promise results?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. I guarantee consistency, a quality process and a transparent monthly report on how things are going.

Where do you work?

Across Italy, and soon internationally. Filming takes place at your premises: I travel with all the equipment, and you don't have to organise anything.
